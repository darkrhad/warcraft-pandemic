# Trial hero render: Tirion Fordring

Goal: see if a "painted miniature" look works as a hero figure on the 2D board.
Original design: a generic old paladin, not a copy of Blizzard's Tirion.

## Hero

> A single tabletop board game miniature figure of an elderly holy paladin knight, a tall
> broad-shouldered old warrior with a long white beard and short white hair, stern noble face,
> wearing heavy ornate silver plate armor with gold trim and large rounded pauldrons, a white and
> blue tabard with a golden sun emblem on the chest, a long blue cape flowing behind him. He holds
> a huge two-handed greatsword point down in front of him with both hands on the hilt, the blade
> glowing with soft golden holy light. Heroic standing pose, full body from head to feet, seen from
> a three-quarter front view, slightly from above. He stands on a small round dark stone base with
> frosty snow on it.

## Lich King

> A single tabletop board game miniature figure of a towering undead king of ice and death,
> wearing heavy black and dark steel spiked plate armor with frost on the edges, a tall horned
> spiked crown helmet that hides his face except two glowing cold blue eyes, huge spiked
> pauldrons with skulls, a long tattered dark cape, holding a long jagged runic sword glowing
> icy blue, cold blue mist around his feet. Menacing standing pose, full body from head to
> feet, seen from a three-quarter front view, slightly from above. He stands on a small round
> dark stone base with ice and frost on it.

## Style

> Hand-painted collectible miniature, highly detailed sculpt, crisp painted edges, rich saturated
> colors, soft studio lighting from the top left, the whole figure in frame with space around it,
> centered, isolated on a plain flat pure white background, no scenery.

## Negative

> text, letters, watermark, signature, frame, border, background scenery, landscape, floor,
> table, multiple figures, cropped, cut off feet, cut off head, blurry, extra limbs, extra arms,
