Hero figure on a sketch board

Trial render of Tirion Fordring (Draw Things, DreamShaper XL Turbo). Click a space to move him. Board layout and links are made up.

  • All 4 versions come from the same prompt (PROMPT.md) with different seeds. Background cut out with macOS Vision.
  • The figure is anchored at its base, so it stands on the space and overlaps what's behind it.
  • Icecrown: 3 renders (ICECROWN.md): tower, closed base, open base. The closed base is drawn twice, split at the pit line, so the tower sinks behind the front wall. Shake, cracks, shards and flash are code.
  • Lich King: same miniature prompt style (PROMPT.md, version 1 of 4). He stands on the dashed Lich King spaces, moves with "Lich King moves", and goes to Icecrown when it opens.
  • The flat pawn is the other option: a colored disc with a class icon, which is easier to read when spaces are crowded.
