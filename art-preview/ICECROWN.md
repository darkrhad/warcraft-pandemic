# Trial render: Icecrown Citadel

Three pictures in the same miniature style as the hero. The page splits the closed base
into a back half and a front half (clip), so the tower can sink behind the front wall.

## Style

> Hand-painted collectible tabletop miniature terrain piece, highly detailed sculpt, crisp
> painted edges, rich saturated colors, icy blues and pale steel greys with cold glowing
> cyan accents, soft studio lighting from the top left, seen from a three-quarter view slightly
> from above, the whole piece in frame with space around it, centered, isolated on a plain
> flat pure white background, no scenery.

## tower

> A single tall slender gothic spire of a frozen undead fortress, dark steel and ice, sharp
> jagged points and spikes, many thin pointed side spires around one very tall central spire,
> glowing cold cyan windows, frost and icicles, the bottom of the tower is a flat cut, no base,
> no walls, no ground, tall and narrow, vertical.

## base

> A round frozen fortress base seen from slightly above: a low circular ring of dark steel and
> ice battlement walls with spiked crenellations and small gate towers, the inside of the ring
> is an empty flat round courtyard of ice, nothing in the middle, snow on the walls, wide and
> short, horizontal.

## open

> A round frozen fortress base seen from slightly above: a low circular ring of broken dark
> steel and ice battlement walls with spiked crenellations, the walls cracked and open at the
> front with a gap, in the middle of the round icy courtyard a frozen throne on steps with a
> glowing cyan magic circle around it, swirling cold mist, wide and short, horizontal.
